What the walkthrough shows

A guided tour of the existing local pilot workspace, using only synthetic data.

  • One workbench

    Nimbir brings revenue-cycle work into a single workbench, so queue review happens in one place rather than across disconnected screens.

  • Per-hospital work queues

    Each hospital workspace has its own authenticated work queue, kept separate per tenant.

  • Status & due dates

    Items carry a status and a due date, so you can see what’s open, in progress, or done, and what needs attention next.

  • Audit history

    Every action in the synthetic workspace leaves an audit-history trail you can inspect during the walkthrough.

What stays human-reviewed

This is a local pilot, not deployed production. Nimbir does not accept or upload your data for this walkthrough, and decisions in the pilot workspace are demonstrated on synthetic records only. Any real deployment, scope, and safeguards are agreed with a person first — we start with a conversation, not a form.

Production PHI handling and BAA coverage, live EHR and clearinghouse integrations, and service-level agreements are not included in this pilot unless separately agreed.
